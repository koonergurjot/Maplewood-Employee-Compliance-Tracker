How this helper maps to the manifest

The PWA manifest expects square icons named icon-192.svg and icon-512.svg with a dark background.

  • Upload once — the converter resizes and centers your artwork on a square artboard so you can replace icon-192.svg and icon-512.svg.
  • Dual formats — download PNGs for store submissions and matching SVG wrappers that align with the type: "image/svg+xml" entries already in manifest.webmanifest.
  • Color presets — use the manifest theme color (#111827) or switch to a transparent background before exporting.
  • Reminder — commit the generated files to icon-192.svg and icon-512.svg (and optionally PNG siblings) so the service worker can cache them.

Tip: include ample padding in your source artwork. The converter keeps your image centered and scales it down to fit inside the icon frame.
